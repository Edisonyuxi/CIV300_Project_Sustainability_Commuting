1. Information

Combustion engines convert the chemical energy stored in gasoline or diesel into mechanical work to move vehicles. However, a large fraction of this energy is lost as waste heat through the exhaust, engine block, and friction in the drivetrain.

Electric drivetrains, especially when powered by low-carbon electricity, can achieve much higher end-use efficiency because less energy is lost as heat in the motor and gearbox.

Key relationships (placeholders):

  • Energy efficiency: η = Useful Energy Out / Energy In
  • Typical values used in the main page sliders:
    • Internal combustion engine (ICE): ηICE ≈ 20–35%
    • Electric drivetrain: ηEV ≈ 80–95%
  • Relative improvement: Δη ≈ ηEV − ηICE
