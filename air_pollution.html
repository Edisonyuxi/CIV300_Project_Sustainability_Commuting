1. Information

Transport emissions change the chemical composition of the lower atmosphere. Internal combustion engines emit carbon dioxide (CO₂) as well as local air pollutants such as nitrogen oxides (NOₓ), particulate matter (PM₂.₅, PM₁₀), carbon monoxide (CO), and volatile organic compounds (VOCs). These pollutants contribute to smog formation and adverse health effects.

Public transit and active modes generally emit less pollution per passenger-kilometre than single-occupancy cars, especially when powered by cleaner electricity mixes.
