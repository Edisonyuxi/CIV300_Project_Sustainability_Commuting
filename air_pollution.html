1. Information

CO₂ & local pollutants · Toronto private vehicles

Transport emissions change the chemical composition of the lower atmosphere. Internal combustion engine vehicles (ICEVs) emit carbon dioxide (CO₂) from fuel combustion, and also local pollutants such as NOₓ, PM₂.₅, CO, and VOCs that contribute to smog and health impacts.

In this page, we focus on annual CO₂ emissions from different vehicle classes in the Toronto region, using typical fuel consumption (L/100 km), annual distance travelled (km/year) and fuel emission factors (kg CO₂/L). We then combine these with an estimate of how many vehicles of each type are on the road.

Per-vehicle annual CO₂ model

  • Fuel emission factor (EF): gasoline ≈ 2.3 kg CO₂/L, diesel ≈ 2.7 kg CO₂/L.
  • Annual emissions per vehicle: CO₂ (t/vehicle·year) = d × FC × EF / 1000 where d = annual distance (km/year), FC = fuel consumption (L/100 km).
  • City-wide emissions for each category: CO₂total = CO₂per vehicle × number of vehicles.

The tables below show a stylized estimate for Toronto (2024), based on Canadian federal data and a simple breakdown of gasoline cars, SUVs, sports/2-seaters, diesel vehicles, and motorcycles.
