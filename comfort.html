1. Information

Human thermal comfort depends on a combination of air temperature, humidity, wind speed, radiation, clothing and activity level. In urban streets and transit corridors, heat exposure can be amplified by the urban heat island effect.

Common metrics used in this course include the heat index or the Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI), which combine meteorological variables into a single “feels-like” temperature.

Example simplified relationship (placeholder):

  • Apparent temperature used in the main page: Tapp ≈ T + f(RH) − g(Wind) + ΔTUHI where:
    • T = air temperature (°C)
    • RH = relative humidity (%)
    • Wind = wind speed (m/s)
    • ΔTUHI = urban heat island temperature increase
