1. Information

Urban heat island (UHI) describes the tendency for built-up areas to be warmer than surrounding rural regions. Paved roads, rooftops, and parking lots often have low albedo and high heat capacity, so they absorb and store more solar energy during the day and release it as sensible heat at night.

In this project, we focus on how transportation infrastructure (roads, parking, transit corridors) changes the local surface energy balance and affects UHI intensity.
